Eusate builds AI customer-support software for businesses. Its AI support agent is called Sate. A business gives Sate its own knowledge (help articles, FAQs, documents, web pages), and Sate answers its customers' questions on channels such as website chat, WhatsApp and email, with a helpdesk where human agents can step in. Businesses can also connect Sate to their own systems so it can look up live information.
