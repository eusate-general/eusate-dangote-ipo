title: How subscribing generally works
reviewed: false
---
You apply through an officially approved receiving agent or subscription channel: a bank, a stockbroker, or an investment or payments app. Eusate is not one of them and cannot subscribe for you.

Typical steps, which vary by platform:
1. Pick a channel from the reported approved list and confirm it on the official site (ipo.dangote.com).
2. Create or log in to your account and complete identity verification. This generally needs your BVN and bank account details.
3. You need a CSCS account (or stockbroking details) to hold the shares. Some platforms can create one during subscription.
4. Choose how many shares to apply for. The reported minimum is 10 shares. Use the calculator tools for amounts.
5. Pay through the platform's own payment flow, and keep the confirmation.

Check your platform's own cut-off time, which may be earlier than the official closing date. Only pay inside the platform's official app or website, never to a personal account or to someone who contacted you.
