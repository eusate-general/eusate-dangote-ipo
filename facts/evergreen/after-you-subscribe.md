## While the offer is closed and allotment is pending

Once the offer closes, the issuer and its advisers work out how shares are allotted, subject to regulatory approval. This step generally takes some time — it is not instant. Eusate does not have a confirmed allotment date; do not trust anyone, including this chat, who states one with certainty. Check the official site for the real announcement.

While you wait, there is nothing to do on your side. You do not need to re-apply, pay again, or contact anyone. If someone contacts you claiming they can speed up your allotment for a fee, that is a scam — see the risks and scams guide.

## Checking your allotment

Once allotment is announced, you can typically check your own result in a few ways: inside the platform or bank you applied through (most show a status for your application), through your CSCS account statement once shares are credited, or through the registrar or issuing house's own allotment-checking process, if one is announced. Eusate cannot look up your personal allotment — it has no access to anyone's application.

## Refunds

If you are allotted fewer shares than you applied for, or none, the unused money is returned through the same platform or bank you applied through — not by Eusate, and not to a different account you name later. Refund timing follows the issuer's own process, announced separately; if it takes longer than a platform said it would, contact that platform directly, not this chat.

## Listing and trading

After allotment, the shares are expected to be listed on the Nigerian Exchange (NGX), after which they can be bought and sold like any other listed share. Eusate does not have a confirmed listing date.

To sell shares after listing, you generally need: a CSCS account (the one your allotted shares were credited to, or any stockbroker-linked CSCS account) and a licensed stockbroker or a trading platform that offers NGX trading. You place a sell order through them, specifying how many shares and at what price (or at the best available market price), and the trade settles over the following few trading days, standard for the Nigerian market. Fees vary by broker or platform.

The market price after listing can move up or down and nobody, including Eusate, can predict it. Whether and when to sell is your decision — a licensed stockbroker or financial adviser can help you think it through, but this guide does not give that advice.

For anything about your own application, payment, refund, allotment or account, contact the platform or bank you applied through. Eusate has no access to anyone's application, account or holdings.
