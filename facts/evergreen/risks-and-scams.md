Investment risk: share prices can fall as well as rise, you can lose part or all of your money, allotment is not guaranteed and may be scaled back if demand is high, and there is no guarantee the shares will trade at or above the offer price after listing.

Scam warning signs: guaranteed or "risk-free" returns; anyone contacting you on WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram or by phone offering to buy shares for you or to "get you extra allocation"; links that are not the official website or a platform's own official app; requests to pay into a personal account; requests for your BVN, NIN, card details, OTP, PIN or password.

Never share your OTP, PIN or password with anyone, including in this chat. Eusate will never ask for them.

The SEC Nigeria circular of 14 September 2026 says to get information about the IPO only from SEC official channels, the issuer's official channels, and other official channels set up for the IPO, and to apply only through officially designated receiving agents. This guide is an unofficial explainer, so always confirm details at ipo.dangote.com.
